# Store listing texts

Copy these into **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**. The limits are Play's.

## App name (30 characters max)

```
MediaSync: Photo Sync & Backup
```

## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Copy photos and videos from your phones to one phone you own. Private, no cloud.
```

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
MediaSync copies the photos and videos from your family's phones to one phone you choose, such as an old Pixel on a charger at home. They go directly from phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted. No account, no ads, no analytics, and nothing passes through a MediaSync server.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a receiving phone and pair each sending phone with it once, by scanning a QR code.
• Every night while the phones charge (at 3:00 unless you choose another time), new photos and videos are copied over Wi-Fi. Or tap Sync now at any time, also from the Quick Settings tile or the home-screen widget.
• Share photos and videos to MediaSync from any gallery app, and they're sent first, right away.
• Everything arrives exactly as taken: the same bytes, with date, time zone, place, camera details, album and file name.

YOU DECIDE WHAT'S SENT
• Choose the folders: Camera only, Camera and Screenshots, all folders, or your own pick.
• Optionally only photos taken from a first day, until a last day, or between the two, both days included.
• Choose the order: newest first, oldest first, photos first or videos first, or let the phone decide.
• Photo details: keep everything, remove the place, or remove all details before a photo leaves the sending phone.

FAST
• Several streams in parallel use your Wi-Fi to the full.
• Turbo links two phones directly, with Wi-Fi Aware where both phones have it and Wi-Fi Direct otherwise, even without a shared Wi-Fi network: ideal for the first big copy.

BACKED UP BY GOOGLE PHOTOS, IF YOU LIKE
• Received photos go into one folder that the Google Photos app on the receiving phone can back up. On a Pixel 2 to 5 that's free and unlimited in Storage saver quality.
• Photos and videos without a date inside, such as WhatsApp images, get their date written in first, so they land on the right day.
• When Google Photos frees up space, MediaSync shows them as backed up. It never deletes anything itself.

ALSO OVER THE INTERNET (OPTIONAL)
• Phones away from home sync through your own free Cloudflare account, set up from the receiving phone in a minute.
• Phones connect directly when the networks allow it; otherwise through Cloudflare's relay, which can't read your photos. A meter shows the free monthly allowance, and MediaSync never goes past it. Videos wait for Wi-Fi unless you let them use the relay.
• Mobile data only if you allow it, with an optional monthly cap.

YOURS TO MANAGE
• Remove a sending phone from the receiving phone, or unpair a sending phone and pair it with another one. What was sent always stays.
• Reset MediaSync to start over at any time.
• In English, Czech, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Slovak, Spanish and Ukrainian, and built for TalkBack and large text.

OPEN SOURCE
• MediaSync is open source under the Apache 2.0 license: github.com/danyk20/mediasync
```
